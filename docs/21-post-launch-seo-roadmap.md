## 1.1 Build-First Post-Launch Governance

Post-launch research, briefs, templates, candidate routes, content production, optimization experiments, and protected-preview builds may proceed continuously. They do not require pre-build permission from a prior performance cohort.

The governing principle is:

> **Business truth stays strict. Development stays flexible. Publication is deliberate. Indexation is quality-controlled.**

Post-launch growth uses three independent states:

| State | Roadmap behavior |
|---|---|
| Development | Research and build opportunities in bounded, manageable work batches |
| Production publication | Release complete routes deliberately according to business priority and operational readiness |
| Indexation | Make only quality-qualified, differentiated routes eligible for organic discovery |

Performance data informs all three states but does not automatically publish, index, delete, consolidate, redirect, or retire a route. The Master Page Build List records production and indexation decisions; it is not permission to begin development.

# 2. Roadmap Objective

The objective after launch is not to immediately publish the largest possible number of pages.

The objective is to turn the new website into a progressively stronger search and lead-generation asset through controlled expansion.

The governing model is:

```text
Launch
    ↓
Stabilize
    ↓
Measure
    ↓
Learn
    ↓
Improve
    ↓
Expand
    ↓
Measure Again
```

Growth should be based on:

```text
Business Priority
+
Search Opportunity
+
Existing Performance
+
Content Quality
+
Market Relevance
+
Conversion Potential
```

        ↓

# 5. Primary Growth Levers

Post-launch SEO growth should come from seven primary levers.

## Lever 1 — Improve Existing Pages

Increase performance of pages already earning impressions, clicks, or leads.

## Lever 2 — Expand High-Value Service Coverage

Develop deeper authority around the most commercially important services.

## Lever 3 — Expand Geographic Coverage

Publish selected location and service + location pages based on real opportunity.

## Lever 4 — Expand Audience Coverage

Develop content for users with distinct conversion intent.

## Lever 5 — Build Topic Authority

Create resources that support services, markets, audiences, and decisions.

## Lever 6 — Strengthen Local Entity Signals

Improve GBP, citations, reviews, and market-specific proof.

## Lever 7 — Earn External Authority

Develop citations, links, mentions, partnerships, and useful reference assets.

# 30. Location Publication and Indexation Stop Conditions

Pause or reduce production publication and indexation—not necessarily research or development—if released cohorts show:

* poor indexation
* no meaningful impressions
* heavy cannibalization
* low content differentiation
* no conversions
* doorway-page patterns
* weak business relevance

The correct response to poor performance is not automatically more pages.

# 135. Protect / Improve / Expand Framework

Strong performance. Avoid unnecessary major changes.

Existing opportunity but underperformance.

Strong signals justify supporting content.

Intent overlap or weak differentiation.

No strategic value remains.

Insufficient evidence yet.
