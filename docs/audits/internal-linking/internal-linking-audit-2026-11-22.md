The shared FAQ linker now skips text between double quotation marks (straight or curly), inside q, blockquote and cite elements, and inside any element marked data-no-link. The Chula Vista answer that reported the City brochure ("contact a licensed plumber or sewer cleaning contractor") was linking "sewer cleaning" to our Sewer Cleaning page; that sentence is now wrapped in a data-no-link span and carries no link. A scan of the built FAQ output found one other link inside quotation marks, the Services hub answer that quotes the terms "sewer scope" and "sewer camera inspection"; the new rule removes it. `scripts/verify-internal-links.mjs` now fails if an FAQ answer link sits inside quoted or marked third-party text.

## 7. Decisions and open questions

- FAQ answers are authored as JSX, not strings. Instead of adding a markdown-link format, the existing linker (`lib/links/faq-links.tsx`) was extended; JSON-LD is already derived from the same JSX (`lib/schema/faq.ts`), so text cannot drift, and the gate now verifies it from the built HTML.
- Linker limits: first mention of a target per page, 2 links per answer (3 only when an answer names several markets), never inside questions, never to the page itself, no cross-market links from a market page, "City of X" is not treated as a market mention, location links only from market, resource and location pages.
- No open questions that change business facts.
