## 1. Purpose

This document defines the approved business positioning, messaging framework, service boundaries, customer value propositions, brand principles, and content guardrails for **The Sewer Pros** website.

It exists to ensure that:

* website content accurately represents the business
* service pages remain consistent
* local pages use the same core positioning
* AI-generated content does not invent services
* sales messaging does not drift into unsupported claims
* The Sewer Pros is differentiated from repair-driven sewer contractors
* future markets can use the same brand foundation
* Claude and Claude Code have a reliable source of truth for business messaging

# 2. Business Identity

## 2.1 Business Name

**The Sewer Pros**

Use this as the primary brand name unless a verified market-specific legal or DBA name requires otherwise.

Do not create alternate brand names for individual markets without explicit approval.

---

## 2.2 Business Category

The Sewer Pros is best positioned as a specialized sewer and drain inspection, diagnostics, cleaning, and locating company.

The business should not be broadly described as a general plumbing company unless specifically approved.

Primary conceptual categories include:

* sewer inspection company
* sewer camera inspection service
* sewer diagnostics company
* sewer cleaning service
* hydro jetting service
* sewer line locating service
* drain cleaning service
* pre-purchase sewer inspection service
* commercial sewer and drain service

Exact third-party platform categories, including Google Business Profile categories, will be governed separately.

# 3. Core Business Positioning

The Sewer Pros helps property owners, buyers, real estate professionals, property managers, and commercial customers understand and maintain sewer and drain systems through specialized inspection, diagnostics, locating, and cleaning services.

The core positioning is:

> **Specialized sewer inspection, diagnostics, locating, and cleaning without repair-driven upselling.**

The company should be presented as a specialist focused on identifying the condition of sewer and drain systems, documenting findings, cleaning systems where appropriate, and helping customers understand what is happening underground.

The business model should be differentiated from companies whose primary revenue opportunity may come from selling major sewer repair or replacement projects.

---

# 4. Primary Differentiator

## 4.1 Independent Inspection and Diagnostics

A central differentiator is that The Sewer Pros can provide sewer inspection and cleaning services without making major sewer repair or replacement the core objective of the inspection.

This supports a customer-first message built around:

* inspection
* documentation
* diagnostics
* transparency
* cleaning
* locating
* evidence
* informed decision-making

The intended perception is:

**The Sewer Pros helps determine what is actually happening before a customer commits to a major sewer decision.**

---

## 4.2 Messaging Concept

Where appropriate, content may communicate ideas such as:

* Get the sewer line inspected before assuming it needs replacement.
* Understand the condition of the sewer line before making a major decision.
* See what is happening inside the sewer line.
* Get objective documentation of sewer conditions.
* Identify the location and condition of the line.
* Clean and reassess the system when appropriate.

# 6. Approved Core Service Positioning

The website should presently center the business around the following service concepts.

Use camera technology to visually inspect the inside of sewer lines and help identify visible conditions, obstructions, defects, or areas requiring further evaluation.

Primary value:

* visibility
* documentation

## 6.2 Sewer Inspection

A broader inspection service for evaluating the condition and function of a sewer line.

Depending on the approved service definition, this may include:

* camera inspection
* visual findings
* locating
* documentation
* recommendations for next steps

Do not imply structural engineering or municipal certification unless specifically applicable.

## 6.3 Sewer Diagnostics

Focused investigation designed to determine the likely cause or location of sewer-related problems.

Potential problems may include:

* recurring backups
* slow drainage
* blockages
* root intrusion
* line defects
* offsets
* bellies
* buildup
* unknown sewer routing

Any specific diagnostic capability should be confirmed in the Master Service Registry before being represented as guaranteed.

---

## 6.4 Sewer Cleaning

Cleaning intended to restore or improve flow by removing approved types of buildup, obstructions, or debris.

The precise cleaning methods and limitations should be defined in the canonical service records.

---

## 6.5 Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water cleaning used for appropriate sewer or drain applications.

Potential positioning includes:

* heavy buildup removal
* grease removal
* recurring blockage treatment
* commercial drain maintenance
* sewer line cleaning

Do not describe hydro jetting as universally appropriate for every pipe or condition.

---

## 6.6 Sewer Line Locating

Locating the approximate path or position of sewer lines using approved inspection and locating equipment.

Potential use cases include:

* understanding sewer routing
* identifying problem areas
* locating inspection findings
* real estate evaluations
* property planning
* supporting further contractor evaluation

Avoid implying survey-grade precision unless that capability is specifically verified.

---

## 6.7 Drain Cleaning

Cleaning and clearing approved drain systems.

Drain cleaning content should remain consistent with The Sewer Pros' specialized sewer and drain positioning rather than turning the brand into a broad general plumbing company.

---

## 6.8 Pre-Purchase Sewer Inspection

Sewer inspection performed during or around a property purchase decision.

Primary audiences may include:

* homebuyers
* real estate agents
* investors
* property inspectors
* property managers

The purpose is to provide additional information about sewer conditions before a buyer takes on the financial risk associated with the property.

## 6.9 Commercial Sewer and Drain Services

Inspection, diagnostics, cleaning, locating, and related approved sewer/drain services for commercial properties.

# 13. Messaging Guardrails

## 13.1 Avoid Fear-Based Selling

Do not overuse messaging such as:

* Your home could be in danger.
* Your sewer is about to collapse.
* Waiting will cost you thousands.
* This could destroy your property.
* You need immediate replacement.

Urgency may be appropriate for an active sewage backup or similar condition, but urgency should reflect the actual customer situation.

---

## 13.2 Avoid Unsupported Superlatives

Do not claim:

* #1 sewer company
* best sewer company
* most trusted sewer company
* highest rated
* leading sewer experts
* guaranteed lowest price
* guaranteed diagnosis

unless adequate verified evidence exists and approval is documented.

---

## 13.3 Avoid Attacking Competitors

The independent-inspection positioning should not become:

* repair companies are dishonest
* plumbers intentionally misdiagnose sewer lines
* contractors always recommend unnecessary repairs
* other companies cannot be trusted

Preferred framing:

> A company that specializes in inspection and cleaning can provide another perspective before a customer commits to a major repair decision.

The distinction should be structural and customer-focused rather than accusatory.

---

## 13.4 Do Not Invent Credentials

Do not fabricate:

* licenses
* certifications
* awards
* years in business
* insurance status
* affiliations
* municipal approvals
* inspection certifications
* manufacturer certifications
* equipment brands
* warranties

These facts must come from verified business records or approved project documentation.

# 24. Content Accuracy Standard

Content must distinguish between:

### Confirmed Business Facts

Information verified through company-provided materials or approved project documentation.

### Industry Knowledge

General educational information about sewer and drain systems.

### Market Research

Externally researched information about locations, search behavior, infrastructure, competitors, or customer needs.

### Strategic Recommendations

Ideas proposed for future implementation but not yet approved.

These categories should not be blurred.

For example:

> "Older homes can have aging sewer infrastructure"

may be industry or market information.

But:

> "The Sewer Pros has inspected thousands of older homes"

is a business-specific claim requiring verification.

# 37. Brand Decision Test

When considering new messaging, ask:

1. Is the statement factually verified?
2. Does it align with an approved service?
3. Does it preserve the inspection/diagnostics/cleaning positioning?
4. Does it avoid implying repair services?
5. Does it help the customer understand the problem or decision?
6. Does it avoid unnecessary fear?
7. Does it differentiate The Sewer Pros without attacking competitors?
8. Can the statement be supported consistently across markets?
9. Is market-specific evidence required?
10. Would the statement remain accurate if quoted independently by a search engine or AI system?

If the answer to any material question is no, revise the message before publication.

```text
                                      buttons, focus rings, emphasis
```

18 §29 makes this a discipline rather than a preference. A conversion color
stops reading as one the moment it also appears on icons, borders, and
headings. Green currently appears in exactly three places in the codebase,
all primary conversion buttons.

## Contrast

Verified at implementation, not assumed. Every pair clears WCAG AA (4.5:1):

| Pair | Ratio |
| --- | --- |
| White on CTA green | 5.45:1 |
| White on primary blue | 5.83:1 |
| White on brand dark | 14.23:1 |
| Muted text on white | 5.47:1 |
| Muted text on muted surface | 5.08:1 |
| Body text on white | 14.76:1 |

Re-verify any new pairing before shipping it. A token that is safe on white
is not automatically safe on `--surface-muted` or on the brand surface.
