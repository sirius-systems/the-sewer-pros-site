# 2. Governing Content Principle

Every page must justify its existence.

A route should not be published or indexed merely because:

* a keyword exists
* a service/location relationship exists
* a competitor has a similar page
* a page can be programmatically generated
* the geographic registry contains the location
* the service × location matrix contains the combination

Any of these signals may justify research, a content brief, a candidate route, a draft, or a protected-preview build. They do not by themselves justify production publication or search indexation.

Every indexable page must provide:

```text
Distinct Search Intent
+
Distinct User Value
+
Accurate Business Relevance
+
Sufficient Unique Content
+
Clear Conversion Purpose
```

If these conditions cannot be satisfied, the page must remain non-indexable. It may remain in research, draft, or protected-preview status while the team improves or evaluates it.

# 3. Build-First Content Governance

This document defines how candidate, published, and indexable pages should be written. It does **not** create a pre-build permission gate.

The governing principle is:

> **Business truth stays strict. Development stays flexible. Publication is deliberate. Indexation is quality-controlled.**

The authoritative production and indexation control plane remains:

`04-master-page-build-list.md`

The governing sequence is:

```text
Opportunity Identified
        ↓
Research + Business-Truth Check
        ↓
Content Brief / Candidate Route
        ↓
Draft + Local or Protected-Preview Build
        ↓
Content, Technical, and Release QA
        ↓
Master Page Build List Publication State
        ↓
Production Publication
        ↓
Indexation When Quality-Qualified
```

Research, briefs, templates, drafts, candidate routes, and protected previews may proceed before a production decision. No route should be automatically published or indexed solely from:

* the Master Service Registry
* the Master Location Registry
* the Service × Location Matrix
* a keyword database
* competitor research
* AI recommendations

Development status, production-publication status, and indexation status are separate controls:

| Control | Example states | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Development | Research, Draft, Review, Build Ready, Built | Work may proceed without publication |
| Production | Withheld, Selected, Published, Retired | Controls public release |
| Indexation | Noindex, Eligible, Indexable, Removed | Controls search-engine eligibility |

The Master Page Build List records production and indexation decisions. It does not determine whether research or development may begin.

# 5. Primary Differentiator

The most important content differentiator is:

> **Independent sewer inspection and cleaning without repair-driven upselling.**

The website should consistently communicate that The Sewer Pros is focused on understanding and documenting the sewer problem rather than using inspection as an automatic path toward selling a major repair.

Appropriate concepts include:

* independent sewer inspection
* objective sewer diagnostics
* camera evidence before major decisions
* understanding the condition of the sewer line
* getting a second opinion before approving a major repair
* determining whether cleaning may resolve the issue
* separating diagnosis from repair sales

This positioning should be woven naturally into relevant pages.

It should not be repeated mechanically on every page.

# 18. AEO Answer-First Structure

Where a page answers an identifiable question, provide a direct response near the beginning of the relevant section.

# 19. FAQ Standard

FAQs should address genuine questions associated with the search intent of the page.

They should not exist only for keyword placement.

Good questions include:

* How does a sewer camera inspection work?
* What can a sewer camera see?
* Should I have the sewer inspected before buying a home?
* Can a sewer line be cleaned instead of replaced?
* Do you provide the inspection video?
* When should hydro jetting be considered?
* Can you locate where the sewer line runs?
* What causes recurring sewer backups?

Answers should generally begin with the answer itself.

Avoid opening every FAQ with:

> It depends.

Instead:

> Yes, in many cases a sewer camera inspection is worth performing before buying an older property because it can reveal conditions that are not visible during a standard home inspection.

Then provide qualifications.

---

# 20. FAQ Duplication Rule

Do not place the exact same FAQ block on hundreds of pages.

Localized or audience-specific pages should include questions relevant to that page.

Some foundational questions may naturally overlap, but the complete FAQ set should not simply be copied across every route.

## Verified Exception — DEC-088

`DEC-088` verifies and authorizes exactly two of the claims listed above:

* **Free estimates** — available; ask before scheduling a sewer inspection or cleaning.
* **Same-day availability** — sometimes available Monday–Friday, 8:00am–4:00pm, depending on scheduling.

These are project-specific exceptions to the internal default stated above, granted on owner confirmation recorded 2026-09-01. They are not exceptions to any external platform policy.

`DEC-088` does **not** authorize:

* 24/7 service
* emergency service or emergency dispatch
* guaranteed same-day arrival
* any specific response time
* financing
* warranty

Same-day phrasing must remain hedged — "sometimes available," never "guaranteed," and never implying weekend or 24-hour coverage. It describes availability rather than promising it.

The approval is scoped to the homepage confidence module. `'same-day service'` and `'free estimates'` deliberately remain in `CLAIMS_REQUIRING_VERIFICATION`, which continues to govern every other appearance — service pages, market pages, and CTAs included.

5. appropriate use cases
6. limitations
7. when one may be preferable
8. when inspection is needed
9. neutral conclusion

# 84. Minimum Unique Value Principle

A 2,000-word page can be repetitive.

* user intent
* complexity
* competitive environment
* business relevance
* information available

The objective is **complete intent satisfaction**, not arbitrary length.

# 85. Content Depth Guidance

Substantial market-specific coverage.

Focused but complete local commercial intent.

Enough depth to address distinct audience concerns.

As long as necessary to answer the question well.

Enough depth to explain actual distinctions objectively.

Do not pad pages to reach a target length.
