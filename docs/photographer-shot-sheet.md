# Photographer shot sheet

Real-photo files only. 16 shots, in four groups by where you shoot. Together they fill 535 boxes. Source: [image-shot-list.md](image-shot-list.md). Each shot fills the boxes listed on its line; one good frame per shot is enough, but the framing variants give choices.


Note: the 12 render images are not on this sheet. The equipment shown in those renders is owner-approved (Oct 12, 2026); their briefs and alt text are in [image-shot-list.md](image-shot-list.md).

## Rules for every shot

- No faces. Keep people out of frame, shoot from behind, or crop at the chin.
- No license plates. Remove or crop them, including on trailers and tow vehicles.
- No readable house numbers, street names or addresses. Blur or crop mailboxes and curb numbers.
- No branded lettering. Cover or crop truck, trailer, uniform and equipment logos and stickers. Equipment shows as a plain object; do not photograph model names or spec plates.
- No customer property identifiers: names, documents, vehicles in driveways, distinctive landscaping, "for sale" signs, real-estate branding.
- No text overlays or watermarks, and no on-screen addresses, dates or GPS on any monitor or tablet.
- No repair, excavation or replacement work in frame, and nothing that implies a local office, storefront or branch.
- Do not present any frame as a finding about a real property. These are examples.
- Get customer or property permission before photographing a job, and keep releases on file. Strip EXIF and GPS before delivery.
- Deliver WebP at the stated size, plus the original RAW or full-size JPEG.

Before you shoot, read the "Must NOT appear" line on each shot; it adds to these rules.
