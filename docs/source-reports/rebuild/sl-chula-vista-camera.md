## What changed from the existing body

The old body had two sections (the City runs the sewer; what the policy says about stoppages) and already carried the 48-hour exception with the hedges "does not by itself decide where a stoppage is" and "pages do not say what documentation it accepts". All of that is kept. New: the street-tree burden of proof set against the camera limit (roots visible inside the pipe, not the root system outside), the Connection Point and the permit split of the same line, the City's cleaning and camera goals labelled as covering the public sewer, the City's warning that cleaning can push debris into the public sewer set against the camera-first or cleaning-first question, the posted policy's blank resolution number, and the City's backup and contact instructions, each labelled the City's. The hero intro, meta description, service description and CTA are new. No sentence says our footage meets a City condition.
