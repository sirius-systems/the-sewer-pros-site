# The Sewer Pros — Content Objective Charter

**Document:** `23-content-objective-charter.md`
**Status:** Adopted (business owner directive, 2026-09-03)
**Applies To:** all content work on this project

---

# 1. Purpose

A single formalized objective statement for all Sewer Pros content work: the
**why** that `10-seo-strategy.md`, `12-content-aeo-ai-strategy.md`,
`site-os-strategy-workflows`, and `site-os-page-build-router` already
implement the **how** of.

This charter does not replace or contradict those documents. It is the
anchor to point back to when scoping a new page, brief, or content decision.

---

# 2. The Objective

> Create accurate, locally relevant, answer-first content that satisfies real
> customer intent, demonstrates The Sewer Pros' topical expertise, is easily
> understood by people, search engines, and AI systems, and guides qualified
> visitors toward a low-friction conversion.

---

# 3. What the Content Should Support

* traditional SEO and local-intent search
* GEO, AEO, and LLM citation visibility
* discovery via Google, Bing, Apple, ChatGPT, and Perplexity
* featured snippets, People Also Ask, rich results, and entity recognition
* Google Business Profile and legitimate local-market visibility
* topical authority through connected service, market, audience, and resource content
* measurement through Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, analytics, and conversion tracking

---

# 4. Operating Rule: One System, Not N Versions

Do not produce different versions of the same content for every platform. There
is no "Google version", no "ChatGPT version", no "AEO version".

Build **one** authoritative content and entity system:

* clear, direct answers up front
* semantic HTML
* structured data, per `15-schema-entity-strategy.md`
* internal relationships, per `16-internal-linking-strategy.md`
* verified business facts only, per `01-business-brand-foundation.md` and CLAUDE.md's accuracy rules
* original local value, never city-token substitution

Search engines, AI answer systems, and human readers all draw from this one
system. Optimizing it well for one does not create an obligation to maintain
parallel content for the others.

## Why this holds

Google prioritizes helpful, people-first content
(<https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content>).

ChatGPT discovery requires pages that are accessible and crawlable and that do
not block `OAI-SearchBot`
(<https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq>).

Both point at the same work. Neither rewards a second copy of it.

---

# 5. Expectation Setting

Featured snippets, PAA placement, rich results, and knowledge-panel presence
are **opportunities the content is built to be eligible for, not guaranteed
deliverables**.

Treat wins in these surfaces as evidence the system is working, not as
promised outcomes to report against.

The primary KPIs remain, per `19-analytics-measurement.md`:

```text
qualified search visibility
qualified traffic
qualified leads
```
